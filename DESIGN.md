# Design System & Direction: Neon Aesthetic Portfolio

## Overview & Identity
- **Owner**: ALDI (Full Stack Developer)
- **Concept**: Neon Aesthetic Cyber-Minimalist Portfolio
- **Purpose**: Showcase high-impact software engineering projects with high visual fidelity, clean readability, and zero AI slop.
- **Dial Declaration**: `ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2`
  - *ENERGY 2 (Balanced)*: Vibrant neon highlights grounded on a deep obsidian black background.
  - *RHYTHM 2 (Varied)*: Asymmetric hero with photo and Spotify card, balanced bio grid, dynamic project showcase cards, and high-focus contact console.
  - *MOTION 2 (Smooth Transitions)*: Interactive avatar morph, soundwave equalizer, card hover glows, and responsive lightbox gallery.

## Color Palette (Electric Neon Purple with Rich Glow)
- **Base Background**: Deep Obsidian (`#070712` / `#0e0d1f`) : solid, restful, high-contrast dark foundation.
- **Core 1 - Vibrant Neon Purple**: `#a855f7` (primary buttons, contact card, active states, rich purple neon glow).
- **Core 2 - Bright Electric Violet**: `#c084fc` (hero name highlight, category labels, focused hover borders).
- **Core 3 - Deep Violet Depth**: `#7e22ce` (ambient background depth, card elevation layers).
- **Secondary Accent - Neon Cyan**: `#00d2ff` (Spotify player controls, audio equalizer, tech tags).
- **Background FX**: Procedural Electric Lightning Engine (`#lightning-canvas`) discharging branched purple thunderbolts every few seconds.
- **Text Contrast (WCAG AA Compliant)**:
  - Primary Headings: `#ffffff` (contrast > 15:1 against `#070712`)
  - Body Text: `#e2e8f0` (contrast > 11:1 against `#070712`)
  - Muted Text / Meta: `#94a3b8` (contrast > 5.5:1 against `#070712`)

## Typography
- **Headings & Accents**: 'Space Grotesk', sans-serif : geometric, modern, high personality.
- **Body Text**: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif : clean, effortless readability across devices.
- **Code & Metadata**: 'JetBrains Mono', monospace : technical credibility for stacks and badges.

## Tone & Copy Rules (Zero Slop)
- No em dashes anywhere. Hyphens with spaces, colons, or parentheses only.
- No buzzwords ("revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "seamless", "next-generation").
- No fake numbers or statistics. Only real facts about actual projects built.
- Text kept punchy and concise, prioritizing visual proof (real project screenshots).

## Interactive Standards
- Every button has a tangible destination or state feedback.
- Keyboard navigation (Tab, Enter, Space, Escape) fully supported.
- Form includes live validation, loading state, and toast confirmation.
